# Richard Schaefer — Light Magic Story Books

## Goal
Build a polished, responsive one-page author website that feels like an illustrated extension of *The Leprechaun and the Unicorn*: enchanted countryside, deep blue skies, warm golden light, lush greenery, wildflowers, rainbows, and gentle magic.

## What I’ll build
- Sticky translucent navigation with a refined mobile menu.
- Full-width illustrated opening scene featuring Liam, Sparkle, countryside, rainbow, and subtle atmospheric motion.
- Featured-book showcase centered on a dimensional book presentation and supplied description.
- Seven-story collection using clearly labeled placeholder titles and copy only.
- Character area for Liam, Sparkle, and replaceable supporting-character spaces.
- Interactive illustrated world map with selectable locations and short placeholder story moments.
- Story values, author biography, gallery with lightbox, parent/educator resources, review placeholders, purchase area, newsletter, contact form, and magical footer.
- Responsive layouts and reduced-motion support across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Complete page metadata and accessible semantic structure.

## Visual direction
- Premium illustrated storybook rather than a generic author or product site.
- Deep navy and royal blue balanced with meadow green, gold, warm cream, pink, and lavender.
- Decorative literary display type paired with a highly readable serif body.
- Generated storybook artwork will establish a cohesive visual world because no uploaded cover file is currently available in the project.
- Sparkles, drifting clouds, gentle butterflies, soft glow, and restrained parallax will create cinematic movement without feeling game-like.

## Content safeguards
- No invented official titles, reviews, retailers, awards, links, or biographical facts.
- Missing information remains clearly labeled as placeholder content.
- Forms and purchase links will be presentation-ready but will not submit or sell until services and real destinations are supplied.

## Technical details
- Implement as a single responsive home page with anchored sections.
- Use local generated visual assets and semantic theme tokens.
- Keep interactions client-side: navigation, map reveals, gallery lightbox, and review carousel.
- No account, database, payments, or external services are required for this version.
